A Warm Welcome

Spyglass & Kettle is a traditional pub and restaurant serving great food, quality drinks and a warm welcome to all. Whether you're joining us for a relaxed lunch, a hearty dinner or just a drink at the bar, you'll find a charming setting, friendly faces and a true local atmosphere.
